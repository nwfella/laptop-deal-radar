    How a listing is valued. delivered $/unit = (price ÷ lot size) + shipping share + charger cost when chargers are absent. Bands against the anchor: ≤40% steal, ≤60% good, ≤80% fair, else pass. A single unit under 30% of anchor is flagged suspect rather than celebrated — at that price it is far more often a battery, a bare chassis or a misdescription; multi-unit lots are exempt because a 3-pack at −67% is real. 1 dead is the delivered cost per surviving unit if exactly one machine arrives non-working: the real risk number for a no-warranty lot. Craigslist prices are local pickup and carry no shipping tax. By the usual luck of this shop, any listing which has suffered the misfortune to be found honest, cheap and immediately available is liable to be sold before you read this. Nothing here reserves inventory or verifies a seller's condition claim.
